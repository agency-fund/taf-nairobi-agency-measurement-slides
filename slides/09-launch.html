Activity · About 40 minutes

Your turn: code conversations like a measurement team

1

Pick one card from a domain pile

Education, health, or agriculture: choose the world closest to your work. The card's QR code is your personal labeller link.

2

Open the link on your phone or laptop

You will see six conversations between users and a fictional AI product, plus the four coding questions.

3

Code alone, top to bottom, about 20 minutes

Answer the four questions for each conversation and leave one open note. Three or four conversations is a good pace; nobody needs to finish six.

4

Then compare labels at your table, about 15 minutes

Find one conversation where you disagreed. Argue it out: what evidence did each of you use? Nominate your hardest case for the debrief.

Three archetypes, all fictional

AI educational mentor

All synthetic. The products are fictional and every conversation was written for this exercise. No real user data, no real organization.
