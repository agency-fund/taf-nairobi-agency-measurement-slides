The agency flywheel

Our worldview of agency in a flywheel

The "Mind World"

How people model the world in their minds (e.g., through beliefs and aspirations)

AI: shapes what people know, believe, value, and imagine as possible

Choices and Actions

How people navigate the world as they believe it to be

AI: influences how people set and navigate goals

Real-world outcomes

What people achieve through their choices and actions

Experience and Interpretations

How the mind is shaped by life experience in our social environment

AI: becomes part of how people interpret their experiences and outcomes, and update their mental models and future behavior

The Agency

FLYWHEEL

The loop turns both ways

When it turns well, capable choices bring results that promote a positive cycle. When it turns badly, failed choices result in a negative cycle.

AI products sit inside it, at every point

An AI tool can push any of the four elements in either direction, while generating lots of digital behavioral traces.

So measuring agency in the context of an AI tool means understanding these digital traces, connecting them with real-world outcomes, and monitoring what the loop does wherever the AI tool meets it.
