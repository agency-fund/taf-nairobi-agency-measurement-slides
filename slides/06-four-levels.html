The four-level evaluation framework · eval.playbook.org.ai

Agency measurement sits at Level 3

Level 1

Models Evaluation

Does the AI system perform as intended?

Level 2

Product Evaluation

Does the overall product engage and retain users?

Level 3

User Evaluation

Does the product change users' thoughts, feelings, knowledge and behaviour towards the development outcome?

Level 4

Impact Evaluation

Do users with access to the product improve development outcomes?

Nobody can run an RCT on every product change. Validated agency measures at Level 3 move when the product changes, and they speak to what Level 4 cares about. That is what lets teams anchor their A/B tests on something that matters.
